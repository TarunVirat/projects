# ☕ Global Coffee Trend – Insight for Strategic Expansion

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes **global coffee consumption, production, imports, and exports** from 1990 to 2023 across 94 countries. The goal is to provide **data-driven insights** for coffee chain executives and stakeholders to identify **promising international markets** for expansion, and to understand consumer preferences at both total and per capita levels.

Using **Tableau visualizations**, the project highlights:
- Long-term consumption trends
- Preferred coffee types
- Top producing, importing, and exporting countries
- Per capita consumption patterns to identify high-potential markets

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify countries with **consistent coffee consumption growth**
- Determine whether growth is **population-driven** or a market trend
- Pinpoint **optimal sourcing countries** for coffee beans
- Analyze **consumer preferences** for coffee types (Arabica vs. Robusta)
- Highlight markets with high **per capita consumption**

## 📊 Data Sources
1. **Coffee Distribution Dataset** – Production, imports, and exports by country
2. **Coffee Consumption Dataset** – Domestic consumption units and coffee type preferences
3. **Population Dataset** – Merged for per capita calculations
4. External references:
   - US Coffee Market: Statista
   - Coffee distribution & consumption: Kaggle
   - Population data: data.gov

## 🛠️ Data Processing
- **Cleaning & Transformation**:
  - Transposed year columns into rows
  - Unified data structure for Tableau compatibility
  - Joined population data to coffee datasets
- **Visualization Tools**: Tableau (13 worksheets, 5 dashboards)

## 📈 Analysis Stages
1. **Trend Analysis (1990–2023)** – Line charts for production, consumption, imports, exports
2. **Type Comparison** – Bar charts for coffee type preferences over time
3. **Top 5 Countries** – Horizontal bar charts for production, consumption, imports, exports (1990 vs. 2023)
4. **Geographical Mapping** – Coffee consumption by country with custom bean-shaped markers
5. **Per Capita Consumption** – Tree map visualization to highlight markets with strong per-person coffee culture

## 🔍 Key Findings
- **Production** up 71% since 1990; **consumption** nearly 6× higher
- **Imports** increased **400×**, showing rising global demand
- Arabica dominates production; Brazil leads globally
- Roast & ground coffee is the most consumed type
- Switzerland, Norway, and Canada lead in **per capita** consumption, indicating strong coffee culture

## ⚠️ Limitations
- Missing detailed EU country-level data (1990–2002 aggregated)
- No economic indicators (GDP, CPI) to assess profitability
- No cultural/political data for international expansion feasibility

## 🚀 Future Enhancements
- Include **land costs**, **local taxation**, and **political stability** for targeted expansion strategy
- Add **consumer spending power** and **market entry barriers**
- Integrate **interactive dashboard filters** for dynamic analysis
